Why Developers Choose ZeroQ Over Apache Kafka

ZeroQ is a free serverless message queue alternative to Apache Kafka and AWS SQS. Routes messages peer-to-peer over WebRTC with browser IndexedDB persistence and dead-letter queues.

Key Advantages of ZEROQ

  • Zero Server Cluster Management — No Zookeeper, Kafka brokers, or EC2 nodes to manage
